Block times are not what a merchant feels. We timed 1.48 million paid orders across fourteen networks, split into the time a shopper takes to choose and the time the network takes to confirm. Lightning lands in 46 seconds, Bitcoin in 14 minutes 16, and Solana hides a fat tail behind an excellent median.
