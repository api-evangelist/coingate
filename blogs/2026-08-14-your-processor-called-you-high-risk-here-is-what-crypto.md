Being labelled high risk means a higher rate, a rolling reserve and a longer document list. Crypto removes chargebacks, issuer declines and the exposure a reserve funds. It does not remove KYB, AML screening, sanctions monitoring or MiCA, and this post says exactly where the line sits.
